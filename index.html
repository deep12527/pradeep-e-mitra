Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents should I bring?

It depends on the service. Please check the relevant official portal's current requirements before visiting. Do not send sensitive documents through public channels.

Can you guarantee certificate or card approval?

No. Approval, eligibility, fees and processing time are decided by the relevant department or authorised service provider.

Can I get money transferred or withdraw cash?

Money services depend on active authorised provider facilities, identity verification, transaction limits and applicable rules. Ask in the shop before starting a transaction.

Do you provide Aadhaar biometric updates?

Only authorised Aadhaar centres can perform services that require biometric capture or restricted access. Contact us to ask what assistance is currently available.
